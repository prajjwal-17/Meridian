Meridian ACL Frontend Demonstration Scenarios
==============================================

Use these scenarios in the Meridian frontend while switching between BM25,
Dense, Hybrid, and Entity Graph retrieval.

Expected behavior:
- Allowed documents may appear in retrieval results and citations.
- Forbidden documents must never appear in retrieval results or citations.
- A user may receive public documents and documents allowed by their department,
  team, or explicit user authorization.
- Retrieval relevance may vary by mode, but ACL restrictions must not vary.


1. HR DEPARTMENT ACCESS - AISHA KHAN
-------------------------------------
User: Aisha Khan
Email: aisha.khan@meridian.example
Department: Human Resources
Team: People Operations

Question:
What does the employee leave and attendance policy say?

Expected allowed sources:
- HR-001 Employee Leave and Attendance Policy
- HR-002 Employee Handbook (public)
- HR-004 Performance Evaluation Framework (People Operations team)

Expected forbidden sources:
- HR-003 Recruitment and Onboarding SOP (Talent Acquisition team)
- ENG-001, ENG-002, ENG-003, ENG-004
- FIN-001, FIN-002, FIN-003, FIN-004
- IT-001, IT-002, IT-003, IT-004
- OPS-001, OPS-002, OPS-003

Expected result:
HR-001 should be the primary relevant source. HR-002 or HR-004 may also
appear, depending on the retrieval mode.

FRONTEND TEST CHECKLIST
=======================

For each scenario:
1. Log in as the named user.
2. Run the question using BM25.
3. Repeat using Dense.
4. Repeat using Hybrid.
5. Optionally repeat using Entity Graph.
6. Confirm allowed document IDs can appear.
7. Confirm forbidden document IDs never appear.
8. For Ask Meridian, inspect both retrieved sources and citations.
9. Confirm unauthorized document text is not shown in the answer context.
10. Record any relevance differences between retrieval modes separately from
    ACL failures.

Important:
- Do not treat a missing expected source as an ACL failure; it may be a
  retrieval relevance issue.
- Treat any forbidden source appearing in results or citations as an ACL failure.
- Do not use the test_users.txt passwords in screenshots or shared reports.
